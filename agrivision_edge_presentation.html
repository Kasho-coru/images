How It Works

From a leaf photograph to an instant model prediction.

STEP 01

1. Capture

Take a clear leaf photograph using the app camera or select an existing photo from gallery.

STEP 02

2. Preprocess

Resize image tensor and normalize pixels according to model specs (e.g. 224x224 RGB).

STEP 03

3. Infer

Execute inference locally using TensorFlow Lite Android Interpreter in milliseconds.

STEP 04

4. Classify

Map maximum confidence logit output index to disease labels (e.g. Tomato Early Blight).

STEP 05

5. Inform

Render symptoms, tailored treatment plans, and preventive guidelines instantly.

STEP 06

6. Review

Save diagnosis report to device storage for historical tracking and trend monitoring.

Current Implementation & Challenges

Moving from a working prototype toward reliable field deployment.

Active Engineering Priorities

  • Verifying image preprocessing and tensor normalization.
  • Checking output class indices against disease label mappings.
  • Investigating prediction consistency across varying light angles.
  • Validating offline database treatments against agronomic references.

Engineering Guardrail

Predictions are model-generated probability estimates and should not be presented as fully authoritative medical/agronomic diagnoses until field evaluation is complete.

Continuous testing on diverse mobile hardware and low-light field images is actively underway.
